Watch the heart make the ECG.

Every wave on an ECG is something happening inside the heart. The Visual ECG Learner plays each rhythm beside an animated heart, so you can see which part is depolarizing or repolarizing at every moment.

Before lecture (10 minutes)

  1. Open the lecture's page and read the Preview.
  2. Skim the learning objectives so you know what to listen for.
  3. Play the matching rhythm in the ECG Learner once at ¼ speed.

During lecture

  1. Keep the Follow along tab open beside your notes.
  2. When an ECG is shown, pull up the same rhythm and click the wave being discussed.
  3. Use Medications for quick lookups when a drug comes up.
